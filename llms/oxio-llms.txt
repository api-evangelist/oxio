# OXIO

> OXIO is a Telecom-as-a-Service (TaaS) platform that lets brands and enterprises launch and operate their own mobile networks (MVNOs) through a single API, without building traditional telecom infrastructure. Its API-first platform unifies multiple carriers behind one integration and adds business-intelligence and no-code tooling on top.

OXIO's public developer documentation, OpenAPI definitions, authentication reference, and sandbox are gated behind authorized-customer access, so this file is generated from the API Evangelist catalog profile rather than a published llms.txt.

## Products
- [BrandVNO](https://oxio.com/): No-code platform to build and launch a custom branded mobile service (MVNO in a box).
- [BrandIQ](https://oxio.com/): AI-powered business intelligence over subscriber and network behavior.
- [OXIO Network](https://oxio.com/): Multi-carrier connectivity delivered through a single integration.
